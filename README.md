# StarCrop

### Your Stars, Your Farm, Your Guide.

</div>

## Summary

<p align="justify">
  &emsp;&emsp;<strong>StarCrop</strong> is an offline-first agricultural advisory mobile application that integrates astronomy, weather information, augmented reality (AR), 
  and agricultural knowledge to support informed farming decisions. Designed for smallholder farmers, particularly in rural communities, the application connects observations of the night sky with practical agricultural 
  guidance through location-based sky visualization, constellation identification, cached weather forecasts, and rule-based farming recommendations.
</p>

## Problem

<p align="justify">
  &emsp;&emsp;<strong>Farmers</strong> in rural communities often face challenges accessing reliable agricultural and weather information due to limited internet connectivity and insufficient access 
  to timely farming advisories. Unpredictable weather conditions, including heavy rainfall, flooding, and typhoons, make agricultural planning more difficult. Meanwhile, traditional astronomical knowledge, 
  such as using stars and constellations to recognize seasonal patterns, remains underdocumented and is not easily accessible through modern farming tools.
</p>

## Solution

<p align="justify">
  &emsp;&emsp;<strong>StarCrop</strong> brings together astronomical visualization and agricultural information in a single mobile application. 
  It calculates the position and visibility of celestial objects based on the user's location, date, and time, allowing users to explore the sky and identify constellations through an augmented reality overlay. 
  It also provides access to previously downloaded weather forecasts without requiring an active internet connection and uses agricultural rules to generate practical recommendations for planting, spraying, harvesting, 
  and field preparation. Each farming recommendation includes its information source and verification status. Overall, StarCrop explores the connection between astronomy, weather awareness, 
  and agriculture by transforming sky observations and accessible environmental information into practical farming guidance. 
  It demonstrates how astronomical concepts and mobile technology can be applied to support agricultural knowledge, particularly in communities with limited connectivity.
</p>
